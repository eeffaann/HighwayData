# Qinghai Provincial Expressways

`chnqhs` is a development draft of S20, S22, S63, S101, S201, and S1113.
Waypoint geometry follows OpenStreetMap mainlines, with shaping points between
interchanges. Connections to existing G6 waypoints use exactly the existing
coordinates.

## Numbering and scope

- Retain the signed expressway designation S1113 for Ninggui, rather than
  treating references to the S104 provincial-road corridor as proof of a
  renumbering. Only the built Xining-Tar Monastery section is included.
- S20 follows the Ningke corridor from Xining through Datong and Ketu to Xianmi.
  Its southern section shares the G0611 alignment.
- S63 follows the Chaka-Chahannuo connector. Some OSM ways still carry the older
  S2013 reference; the transport department identifies this connector as S63.
- Do not add the former western S20 and S2013 sections as separate provincial
  routes: the transport department reports those corridors as G0612.
- S201 currently ends at G310 near Guojia. The connection to Gansu S32 and the
  exact southern numbering need further review before extending this draft.
- Planned extensions and ordinary provincial roads are not included. Development
  status is intentional pending review of numbering, termini, and interchange
  labels.

  S101 extent and northern connection to G3032.

  S22 extent and interchanges.

  S201 interchange listing.

  2026: road geometry and interchange locations. Some downloaded snapshots
  predate the query date; construction tags and route references are not treated
  as definitive opening or renumbering notices.
