# Heilongjiang provincial expressways

`chnhls` includes operating provincial expressways, not the unbuilt
2023-2035 provincial expressway plan. The system remains in preview.

| Route | Included section |
|---|---|
| S12 | Bei'an - Fuyu |
| S16 | Yilan - Qitaihe |
| S16 (Mishan) | Mishan - Xingkaihu |
| S18 | Suihua - Daqing |
| S22 | Harbin Zhongyuan Avenue toll plaza - G45 near Zhaoyuan |

The two S16 sections are separate connected routes. The intervening gap
must not be drawn as an operating expressway. The western section continues
past G11 to the Y203 motorway/trunk transition in Qitaihe; the eastern section
starts at G1115 near Mishan and ends at the S641 roundabout. Its Tongxin Road
toll-plaza approach west of G1115 is not included.

S22 ends at its motorway/primary-road transition on Zhongyuan Avenue, not
at G1001. Its planned extension to the Jilin boundary is not included.
Unopened G9901 junctions are not treated as operating exits.

Exit-number labels use explicit own-route OSM junction references where
available. Construction chainages and distances in route tables are not
assumed to be exit numbers. S16 uses crossroad or interchange names where
its own exit numbers could not be established.
