# Heilongjiang national expressways

The November 2026 review centers shaping between mapped carriageways and
uses explicit own-route OSM exit references where available. Road or place
labels remain where an exit number could not be verified. Asian Highway
files were updated to match the national routes on concurrent sections.
User review also moved AH31's northern crossing from the former Heihe ferry
approach to the Heihe road bridge.

Redundant hidden points were removed against a 20-pixel Web Mercator
corridor at zoom 12, retaining all visible points and exact concurrent
waypoint sequences. The separated-carriageway exception described below
uses the paired median trace rather than requiring proximity to both
carriageways.

G1015 includes the Fangzheng - Yanshou - Shangzhi section opened on
January 9, 2026. Its junction with G1011 uses the projected mainline crossing,
not a ramp departure. The continuation north of G1011, including an isolated
motorway-tagged OSM component, is excluded pending confirmation of traffic
opening and a continuous usable alignment; construction acceptance alone
does not establish that it is open.

G1111 shaping follows the intervening corridor where its directional
alignments separate by more than two kilometres north of exit 236. A single
median line there cannot also closely follow both carriageways. Its established
Yichun city approach is retained; the unconfirmed Hegang - Yichun extension
is not included.

The southern G1211 section (`chnhl.g1211har`) follows the signed route from
the Jilin boundary through Lalin to G1001 at Liming. The Harbin approach is
part of this route, not a separate connector. G1211 is not signed along
G9901 toward G1011, so that portion is represented only as G9901.

The northern Bei'an - Heihe G1211 section remains a separate connected
route. Unopened portions of G9901 do not close either route's gaps.
The reviewed G1213 northern endpoint is the G332 junction; G4512 ends at
Nenjiang interchange 687 rather than the untolled approach farther north.

Sources: [G9901 OSM route relation](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/16092441),
[G1015 opening report](https://hlj.cnr.cn/hljyw/20260109/t20260109_527488617.shtml).
The November 2026 geometry review used operating motorway ways, not planned
alignments or construction-completion reports as evidence of traffic opening.
